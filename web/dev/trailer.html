Renders the 30-second announcement trailer frame by frame (1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264) with the app's own renderer and data, entirely in this tab (WebCodecs + a small MP4 writer, no installs). With Sound on, the score (dev/trailer/score.js, synthesised in code and timed to the storyboard) is mixed in as AAC. Rendering is not real time; leave the tab open until it finishes. The title, tagline and URL are set at the top of dev/trailer/storyboard.js.
